Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 September 2026.

TwoPort ("TwoPort", "we", "us") is operated by Khandelwal Waterlink. This page explains what data TwoPort collects and why — there isn't much of it.

The short version

TwoPort sends files, photos, and clipboard text directly between your own Android phone and your own Mac, over your own Wi-Fi network. Nothing you send through the app passes through our servers, and we never see it. There's no account to create and no sign-in. If you buy a licence, we (through our payment providers) keep the minimum needed to issue and support that licence.

What never leaves your devices

Files, photos, and clipboard contents you transfer with TwoPort travel directly between your phone and your Mac over your local network. We don't operate a relay server for this traffic, we don't store copies of it, and we can't read it. The Android app never uploads clipboard or file contents anywhere — the on-device Accessibility permission it can use is only to notice when you've copied something, so it can offer to send it to your Mac; it does not read or transmit the contents anywhere outside the transfer itself.

What we do collect

We don't run analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting inside the TwoPort apps. The only data we hold is what's needed to sell and support licences:

  • Purchases. If you buy TwoPort, our merchant of record, Dodo Payments, collects your email address and payment details to process the transaction and handle tax. We receive your email address and a generated licence key, which we store so we can verify your licence and help you if you lose it. We never receive or store your card details — that's handled entirely by Dodo Payments.
  • Support requests. If you email us, we keep that correspondence to help you, for as long as is reasonably needed.
  • The website. The waitlist and feature-request forms on this site store only what you type into them (e.g. an email if you provide one) so we can follow up or tally votes.

Local network permission

Both apps ask for local network access. This is used only to discover and connect to your own paired devices on your Wi-Fi network — it is not used to browse or scan the wider internet.

Third parties

We use Dodo Payments to process payments and issue licence keys, and Supabase to back the website's waitlist, feature-request and free-licence forms. None of these providers receive the contents of anything you transfer through the app.

Your choices

You can use TwoPort's 7-day trial without ever giving us any information. If you'd like your purchase or support records deleted, email us and we'll remove them, subject to what we're legally required to keep for tax and accounting purposes.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@twoport.app.
