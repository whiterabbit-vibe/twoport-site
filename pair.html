Scan this code from TwoPort

That's the pairing code your Mac shows. Your camera can't pair by itself: TwoPort on this phone does it.

On an Android phone

  1. Get TwoPort from Google Play (free).
  2. Open it and tap Scan code.
  3. Point it at the code on your Mac. That's it.
Get TwoPort on Google Play

On an iPhone

TwoPort's phone app is for Android. From an iPhone you can still send photos to the Mac with Quick Send: on the Mac, click TwoPort in the menu bar, choose Quick Send, and scan that code with the iPhone's camera.
