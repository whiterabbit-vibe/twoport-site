Refund Policy

Last updated 27 September 2026.

If TwoPort isn't working for you, we'd rather refund you than have you stuck with something you're not using.

14-day money-back guarantee

If you're not happy with TwoPort for any reason, email contact@twoport.app within 14 days of your purchase and we'll refund you in full, no questions asked. Refunds are issued to the original payment method through Dodo Payments, and usually appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

After 14 days

Outside the 14-day window, we'll still look at refund requests case by case — for example if the app genuinely doesn't work on your devices and we can't fix it. Email us and explain what happened.

What happens to your licence

Once a refund is issued, your licence key is cancelled. Please remove it from your Macs.

Contact

To request a refund: contact@twoport.app, with the email address you purchased with.
