Your phone on your Mac

6
Browse every folder

Your phone opens in a TwoPort window on your Mac: Camera, Downloads, WhatsApp, every folder.

Search the whole phone

Type a name, or ask for every video on the phone, newest first.

Watch videos without downloading

Double-click a video and it plays straight from the phone. Save a copy only if you want one.

Flip through photos

A gallery view you page through with the arrow keys.

Rename, delete, new folder

Tidy up the phone from your Mac's keyboard.

Several phones at once

Yours, a work phone, the family's. Each one shows up by name.

Moving files

4
Share from phone to Mac

In any app, tap Share, then TwoPort. It lands in your Mac's Downloads a second later.

Send from Mac to phone

Right-click files in Finder, or drop them on TwoPort in the menu bar.

Drag from phone to Mac

Drag photos and files out of the TwoPort window into any folder on your Mac.

Copy on phone, paste in FinderTesting

Press ⌘C in the TwoPort window and ⌘V in any Finder folder. Nothing downloads until you paste. We're still testing this one on more phones.

Photos

3
Back up only new photos

One click brings over what's new since last time. Nothing is copied twice.

Original dates kept

Photos keep the day they were taken, not the day they were copied.

Sorted by month

Into Pictures › TwoPort, one folder per month. Or pick your own folder.

Copy and paste

6
Mac to phone, automatic

Turn it on once on your Mac, then copy as usual. It's already on your phone's clipboard. Off by default, so your phone only gets what you choose to share.

Phone to Mac, automaticTesting

Turn it on once in the phone app, then copy as usual and press ⌘V on your Mac. We're still testing this one on more phones.

Send clipboard button

A button in your phone's quick settings sends whatever you just copied.

Photos and files tooTesting

Copy a screenshot on your Mac and paste it into a chat on your phone, and back. We're still testing this one on more phones.

Clipboard history

Everything you copied on either side, a click away on your Mac.

Passwords never cross

Anything your phone or Mac marks as sensitive, like a password, stays where it is.

Always connected

4
Pair once

Scan a code once. After that, TwoPort finds your phone by itself.

Reconnects by itself

Whenever your phone and Mac are on the same Wi-Fi. Nothing to tap.

Keeps going when screens lock

Lock your phone or your Mac. A transfer carries on.

USB cable, about 4× faster

Plug in and click Use USB once. Unplug any time and it carries on over Wi-Fi.

Friends and other phones

3
Quick Send, no app needed

A friend scans a code with their phone's camera and sends you photos from the web browser.

Works from iPhones tooTesting

From Safari. Big batches from an iPhone are still being tested.

Picks up after a break

If the Wi-Fi drops mid-way, a big batch carries on from where it stopped.

On every plan

  • Nothing goes online: no cloud, no account
  • Encrypted between your phone and your Mac
  • No tracking in the apps
  • Any Android phone with Android 8 or later, any Mac with macOS 14 or later
