And the details

Built for how you actually use two devices.

Import Photos, filed by month

One click brings over new photos and videos with their original dates. Each phone gets its own folder. Stop halfway and it picks up where it left off.

Nothing leaves your room

Phone and Mac talk directly, encrypted, each checking the other's identity. No server, no account. It works with the internet off.

Quick Send, for phones without the app

A friend's phone, or an iPhone. Scan the code on your Mac and a page opens in its browser to send photos or text across. You allow each phone.

Several phones, one Mac

Your phone and your work phone, or the family's. Switch in the menu bar. Shares from any of them arrive on their own.

Clipboard history

Everything that crossed, one click to copy again. Passwords from password managers never cross.

Pause or turn off

Pause from the menu bar, or turn it off on the phone: nothing over Wi-Fi, nothing over the cable.

Instant video

Videos on the phone start playing on the Mac straight away, without copying them first.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Is it a subscription?

    No. Pro is a one-time purchase that covers every 1.x version on up to three of your Macs. If there's ever a 2.0, upgrading will be optional and discounted, and 1.x keeps working.

    What happens when the 14 days end?

    TwoPort keeps working as the Free version: sharing both ways, Quick Send and text on the clipboard. Browsing, Import Photos, the cable speed-up and photos on the clipboard need Pro.

    Does it need the internet?

    No. Phone and Mac talk to each other over the same Wi-Fi network or a USB cable. Nothing goes through a server, and your licence is checked on your Mac.

    Which phones and Macs?

    Android 8 or later and macOS 14 Sonoma or later. We test on Samsung Galaxy phones, from a Galaxy A50 to a Z Fold7.

    Does it work with iPhone?

    The full app is for Android. An iPhone can still send photos and text with Quick Send, from its browser.

    When does it launch, and what does "founder" mean?

    Soon; join the waitlist and you'll get one email when it's out. Founders are the first 100 people on the waitlist who hold the $9 price. They pay $9 at launch, once, and get the same Pro as everyone else.

    I can't afford Pro. Is there another way?

    Yes. Ask for a free licence in the Pricing section, no explanation needed. The free version also stays free, forever.

    My office Wi-Fi doesn't let devices see each other.

    Plug the phone into the Mac with a USB cable. TwoPort uses it on its own.
