    07Questions

    Good to know.

    Do I need a cable?

    No. Your phone and Mac just need to be on the same Wi-Fi. A USB cable is optional: it's faster, and it works where the Wi-Fi keeps devices apart, like some offices.

    Does it show my phone's screen on the Mac?

    No. TwoPort moves things between the two: photos, videos, files and what you copy. Your phone's screen and apps stay on the phone.

    Is it private?

    Yes. Files go straight from your phone to your Mac, encrypted. Nothing is uploaded and there's no account.

    Which phones and Macs?

    Any phone with Android 8 or later, and a Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

    Does it work with iPhone?

    The app is for Android. An iPhone can still send photos and text to your Mac with Quick Send, from its web browser.

    Can a friend send me photos without the app?

    Yes. Your Mac shows a code, they scan it with their phone's camera, and they pick photos in the web browser. It works on Android phones and iPhones.

    Is it a subscription?

    No. The free version stays free. Pro is paid once and covers every 1.x version on up to three of your Macs.
